Why I made the Thanaka Aftershave

I made this for myself, and I used it for a good while before it occurred to me to sell it.

The hair comes back softer, and it comes back slower. That is one person's face rather than a trial, and I would rather say so plainly than dress it up. It is also why the jar exists, and why it is for sale instead of sitting in my bathroom.

The thanaka is the part I keep coming back to. In Myanmar it is ground on a stone every morning and worn on the face from childhood, and it goes on after hair is removed as readily as it goes on against the sun. Burmese women have held for generations that hair coming back under thanaka comes back finer. They were doing it a very long time before anyone thought to ask why it worked.

If you try it, tell me what you notice. I am keeping track.
